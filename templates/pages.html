How scoring works

Only each browser’s latest completed result counts. Each match win earns one point. The top four receive the highest win count plus 1 for fourth, 2 for third, 3 for second and 4 for first, instead of their win points. Equal scores share a rank.

Win rate is wins divided by matches. Different browsers count separately.
