How scoring works

Each match win earns one point. For each completed ballot, take the highest win count as the base: fourth place gets base + 1, third base + 2, second base + 3, and the champion base + 4. These replace their win points, guaranteeing podium order. Other contenders keep their win points, including zero. Equal total points share a rank.

Win rate shows wins divided by appearances, so you can also see how often a contender wins when drawn. Random opponents and repeated entrants in odd rounds still affect results. Anonymous browsers are counted separately, not verified people.
